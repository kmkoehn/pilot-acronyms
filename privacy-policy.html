Privacy Policy

Pilot Acronyms for iPhone, iPad, and Android. Effective November 7, 2026.

This privacy policy covers the Pilot Acronyms app for iPhone, iPad, and Android. The short version: the app does not collect, share, or sell any personal data.

What the app stores

The app saves one setting on your device: the section you last opened, so it can reopen there. This is not personal data, and the app never sends it anywhere.

What the app does not collect

The app has no accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking. It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, files, or any other personal or sensitive data. All of its content is built in, and it works offline.

Who data is shared with

No one, because the app does not collect any data to share.

Links to other websites

Links in the app open in your web browser. Those websites have their own privacy practices, which this policy does not cover.

App stores

Apple's App Store and Google Play handle app downloads and updates under their own privacy policies. They may give the developer aggregated install, usage, and crash statistics, which do not identify you.

Security

No personal data is collected or transmitted, so none is held on any server. The saved setting stays in the app's private storage on your device.

Retention and deletion

The saved setting stays until you uninstall the app, which removes it. On Android, clearing the app's storage also removes it. On iPhone, your device's own backups may include it along with other app data. There is nothing else to delete.

Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this policy's web address with a new effective date.

Contact

For questions about this policy, open an issue at github.com/kmkoehn/pilot-acronyms/issues, or use the support link on the app's App Store or Google Play listing.
