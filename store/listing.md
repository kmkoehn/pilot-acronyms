## Description (both stores)

Pilot Acronyms puts the memory aids private pilot students use in one place, from ARROW and AV1ATE to IMSAFE, PAVE, and MCPRAWN. Each card spells out every letter, explains what it means, and cites the regulation or handbook it comes from.

WHAT'S INSIDE
• Required documents, inspections, and equipment: ARROW, AV1ATE, A TOMATO FLAMES, FLAPS
• Weather: METAR and TAF codes, flight categories, icing, fog, fronts, and thunderstorms
• Airspace classes, special use airspace, speed limits, and transponder rules
• Aircraft systems, V-speeds, weight and balance, and load factors
• Navigation, sectional chart symbols, and compass errors
• Human factors, illusions, risk management, and emergencies
• Airport lights, signs, light gun signals, and radio frequencies
• Key numbers, useful FAA websites, and an abbreviation decoder

STUDY YOUR WAY
• Quiz mode hides the answers until you tap a line
• Search every section at once, by acronym, topic, or regulation number
• Light and dark themes, readable text, and accessible colors
• Works fully offline, with no account, ads, or tracking

Pilot Acronyms is an independent study aid. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the FAA. Always confirm details against the current regulations, the AIM, FAA handbooks, and your aircraft's POH, and study with your flight instructor. Not for flight planning or use in flight.
