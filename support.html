Pilot Acronyms support

Pilot Acronyms is a study aid for private pilot students, for iPhone, iPad, and Android. It explains the acronyms, mnemonics, and key numbers that come up in ground school and on the oral exam.

Report an error or ask a question

Open an issue at https://github.com/kmkoehn/pilot-acronyms/issues. Please include the section and card name, what it says, and where the correct information comes from, such as a 14 CFR section or an AIM paragraph.

Common questions

  • Does it need an internet connection? No. Everything works offline. Links to official websites open in your browser.
  • Do I need an account? No. The app has no accounts, ads, or tracking.
  • How do I hide the answers to quiz myself? Turn on Quiz in the header, then tap a line to reveal its answer.
  • How do I find something fast? Use the search box. It searches every section and the abbreviation decoder.
